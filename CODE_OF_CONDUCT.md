# Community conduct

Discuss public engineering material respectfully. No harassment, discrimination, impersonation, spam, scams, requests for funds or credentials, or disclosure of private information. Do not test private systems without permission. The maintainer may decline contributions or restrict participation. For sensitive security concerns follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never report them publicly. Public Issues are enabled for non-sensitive engineering feedback. This policy does not grant access to Discussions or private channels.
