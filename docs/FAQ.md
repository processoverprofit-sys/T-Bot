# Frequently asked questions

## What is T-Bot?

A privately developed, local-first Solana trading-system research and engineering project. See the [overview](../README.md) and [conceptual architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md). The public repository describes intended responsibilities, not a verified as-built system.

## Can I download it or connect my wallet?

No public software release is available. Do not send funds, connect a wallet or share credentials. This is not an investment service.

## Is it trading live?

No. The public development record does not establish a qualified operational system, and live-money execution is not authorised. See [development status](DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md).

## Are risk checks guaranteed to protect me?

No. The [risk principles](RISK_PRINCIPLES.md) and [threat model](THREAT_MODEL.md) describe intended requirements and residual uncertainty. Market conditions can change, and the effectiveness of private controls is not established by public documentation.

## Where are the actual test or paper-trading results?

The [illustrative tests](TESTING.md) are fictional and unexecuted. Verified paper-trading results are not supplied. The [evidence report template](EVIDENCE_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md) is blank, not a performance report.

## Who independently reviews it?

The work is owner-led and AI-assisted. No completed independent professional audit is claimed. AI review is not independent human assurance.

## Can I contribute or join Discord?

Yes, within the public documentation scope. Read the [contribution policy](../CONTRIBUTING.md), [community feedback](COMMUNITY_FEEDBACK.md) and [central joining guidance](https://github.com/processoverprofit-sys/Processoverprofit-sys/blob/main/docs/COMMUNITY.md#requesting-discord-access). Public Issues and Discussions remain disabled. No verified direct application link has been published.

## How do I report a vulnerability?

Read [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md). Use a confirmed confidential route only; never post sensitive details publicly. Private systems are outside the authorised scope.
